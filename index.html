Clear, expert answers on student visa, spouse visa, and visitor visa — from Bangladesh's most trusted study abroad consultancy since 1998.

To apply for a Canada student visa (study permit) from Bangladesh, you will generally need:

  • Valid passport (minimum 6 months validity beyond your intended stay)
  • University acceptance letter from a Designated Learning Institution (DLI)
  • Study Plan (explaining why you want to study in Canada and your plans after graduation)
  • Statement of Purpose (SOP) for university admission
  • Academic transcripts and certificates
  • English proficiency test result — IELTS, TOEFL, PTE, Duolingo, or MOI certificate
  • Bank statement (proof of sufficient funds)
  • Job profile / employment letter (if applicable)
  • Biometrics enrollment
  • Medical examination results

Document requirements vary by case. SA Associates prepares a personalized document checklist for every student based on their individual profile.

IELTS requirements depend on your program level:

  • Undergraduate: 6.0 overall (no band below 5.5)
  • Postgraduate (Master's): 6.5 overall (no band below 6.0)
  • PhD: 7.0+

Alternative tests accepted by most Canadian universities: TOEFL iBT (79+), PTE (58+), Duolingo (110+), and MOI certificates.

SA Associates advises on the precise requirement for your target institution.

For a Canada student visa, you generally need to show bank statements equivalent to double your total program cost.

Example: If your 2-year Master's program costs a total of BDT 30 lakh, you should ideally show at least BDT 60 lakh in your or your sponsor's bank statements.

This amount covers tuition fees, living expenses, and return travel. The exact requirement depends on your chosen institution, program duration, and province.

SA Associates calculates the correct amount based on your specific university and program.

A Study Plan is a written document submitted with your Canada visa application. It explains to the visa officer:

  • Why you want to study in Canada specifically
  • Why you chose your particular university and program
  • How the degree aligns with your career goals
  • Your plans to return to Bangladesh after completing your studies

A weak or vague study plan is one of the most common reasons for Canada student visa rejection.

SA Associates prepares professionally written, personalized study plans that address all visa officer concerns directly.

No — an SOP (Statement of Purpose) and a Study Plan are two different documents:

  • SOP is for university admission. It highlights your academic background, achievements, research interests, and why you want to join that specific university and program.
  • Study Plan is for the visa application. It focuses on your reasons for studying in Canada, how the education serves your career goals, and your intent to return to Bangladesh.

Both documents are equally important, and both must be carefully tailored. SA Associates prepares both documents for every student we work with.

A job profile (or employment letter) is required when you or your financial sponsor is currently employed. It serves two important purposes:

  • It proves that your sponsor has a stable income and can financially support your education abroad.
  • It demonstrates strong ties to Bangladesh — showing the visa officer that you have reasons to return home after your studies.

If you are a recent graduate or your sponsor is a business owner, alternative documents such as business registration certificates or bank statements can be submitted instead.

For Bangladeshi students applying from outside Canada, the average processing time is approximately 4 to 12 weeks after submitting a complete application:

  • 50% of applications are processed within 4 weeks
  • 90% of applications within 12 weeks

Processing time can vary based on:

  • Completeness of your application
  • Biometrics and medical exam completion
  • Visa office workload (peak seasons: May–August, December–January)

SA Associates advises students to apply as early as possible — ideally 3 to 4 months before their intended intake.

Tuition Fees (Annual):

  • Undergraduate: CAD 13,000 – CAD 29,000
  • Master's: CAD 17,000 – CAD 30,000
  • MBA: CAD 30,000 – CAD 40,000

Living Expenses: CAD 15,000 – CAD 20,000 per year

SA Associates provides a detailed cost breakdown based on your chosen university, program, and city.

The most common reasons Canada student visa applications from Bangladesh are rejected include:

  • Weak Study Plan — not convincing the visa officer of genuine study intent
  • Insufficient financial proof — bank statements that don't meet the required amount or lack transaction history
  • Poor ties to Bangladesh — not demonstrating a clear reason to return after studies
  • Incomplete or inconsistent documents — missing papers or mismatched information
  • Weak SOP — unclear academic purpose or career goals
  • Questionable intent — visa officer not convinced the applicant will leave Canada after the program

SA Associates reviews every application carefully to identify and address these risk factors before submission.

Biometrics enrollment involves providing your fingerprints and a photograph as part of your Canada visa application. It is a mandatory step for most applicants.

In Bangladesh, biometrics can be enrolled at the Canada Visa Application Centre (VAC) in Dhaka, operated by VFS Global.

You will receive a Biometric Instruction Letter (BIL) from IRCC after submitting your online application, which you must present at the VAC to book your appointment.

Canada has three main intakes:

  • September (Fall) — Largest intake, most programs available. Best for university options.
  • January (Winter) — Second largest. Good for students who miss September.
  • May (Summer/Spring) — Smallest intake, fewer programs available. Best for college diplomas.

For most Bangladeshi students targeting Canadian universities, September intake is recommended.

SA Associates advises starting the application process at least 6 months before your target intake date.

You may be eligible for a Canadian Spouse Visa (also known as an Open Work Permit for spouses) if your husband or wife is:

  • Currently studying at a designated Canadian university or college full-time
  • Enrolled in a program that is at least 6 months long

Eligibility also depends on your spouse's specific study permit conditions and the type of institution they are attending.

SA Associates has successfully processed 550+ spouse visa applications and can assess your eligibility in a free consultation.

For a Canada tourist/visitor visa from Bangladesh, you will typically need:

  • Valid passport
  • Completed online visa application (IRCC portal)
  • Recent passport-size photographs
  • Proof of financial support (bank statements)
  • Employment letter or proof of business ownership
  • Proof of ties to Bangladesh (property, family, job)
  • Purpose of visit letter / invitation letter (if visiting family)
  • Travel history (previous visas are a strong advantage)
  • Biometrics enrollment

A strong visitor visa application clearly demonstrates that you have sufficient funds, a genuine reason to visit, and strong reasons to return to Bangladesh.

A Canada visitor visa rejection is not permanent. Here is what to do:

  • Read your refusal letter carefully — it will indicate the reason for rejection
  • Do NOT reapply immediately with the same documents
  • Identify the weakness — insufficient funds, weak ties, unclear purpose?
  • Strengthen your application — better bank statements, stronger employment proof, clearer travel intent
  • Reapply with a complete, well-prepared application

SA Associates reviews rejected applications, identifies the exact reasons for refusal, and helps build a stronger reapplication. There is no appeal process for visitor visas — the best path forward is a better application.

The DS-160 is the mandatory online US nonimmigrant visa application form. It covers:

  • Personal information
  • Travel history
  • Education and employment
  • Security questions

Errors or inconsistencies on the DS-160 can lead to visa denial.

SA Associates guides students through accurate, complete DS-160 completion.

The SEVIS fee is USD 350, paid online at fmjfee.com before your visa interview.

It is separate from the visa application fee of USD 185.

Both fees are mandatory and non-refundable.

SA Associates guides students through both payment processes.
