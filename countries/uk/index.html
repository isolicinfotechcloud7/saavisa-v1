What You Need

Admission Requirements

Complete checklist for Bangladeshi students
applying to UK universities.

Academic Requirements

  • HSC/A-Levels for undergraduate entry; many universities require a foundation year after HSC
  • Bachelor's with a 2:1 equivalent (roughly CGPA 3.0+) for most taught master's programmes
  • Completed SSC, HSC and Bachelor's transcripts (translated & attested)
  • GRE/GMAT rarely required — most UK master's programmes do not ask for them
  • 2 academic or professional references
  • Personal Statement (undergraduate, via UCAS) or Statement of Purpose (postgraduate)

English Proficiency

  • IELTS UKVI (Academic): 6.0–6.5 overall typical, 7.0+ for law, medicine and top programmes
  • Degree-level study requires CEFR B2 in all four skills for the visa
  • Pre-sessional English courses available if you fall just short
  • PTE Academic UKVI and Duolingo accepted by many (not all) sponsors
  • Some universities accept MOI from Bangladeshi institutions — confirm case by case

Financial Requirements

  • First-year tuition (less any deposit already paid) plus living costs for up to nine months
  • Living-cost thresholds are set separately for London and outside London — confirm the current figures on GOV.UK before you apply
  • Funds must sit in the account for 28 consecutive days, ending no more than 31 days before you apply
  • Bank statement, building society book or official loan letter — the format rules are strict
  • Sponsor letter and relationship proof if a parent or relative is funding you
  • Scholarship or university award letter, where applicable

Other Documents

  • Valid passport with at least one blank page
  • CAS (Confirmation of Acceptance for Studies) from a licensed student sponsor
  • Tuberculosis test certificate from an IOM-approved clinic in Dhaka — mandatory for Bangladesh
  • ATAS certificate for certain science and engineering postgraduate courses
  • Biometrics appointment at VFS Global Dhaka
  • Academic Technology Approval, parental consent and birth certificate if under 18
  • CV and work experience letters for postgraduate applications

Work Rights

What You Can Do During & After Studies

What Student Route holders are permitted to do
during their studies and after graduating.

During Studies

20 hrs/week

20 hours a week during term time and full-time during official vacations. Degree-level students may work on or off campus, but self-employment and professional sport are not permitted.

After Graduation

Up to 2 years

Two years of open work permission for bachelor’s and master’s graduates, three for PhDs. From 1 January 2027 the bachelor’s and master’s allowance falls to 18 months, so intake timing matters.

Longer-Term Route

Skilled Worker

Most graduates switch from the Graduate Route to an employer-sponsored Skilled Worker visa, which counts towards Indefinite Leave to Remain. There is no lottery, but you do need a licensed sponsor and a role meeting the salary threshold.

FAQ

Common Questions About Studying in UK

Quick answers to common questions
from Bangladeshi students.

What documents are required for a UK student visa from Bangladesh?
A valid passport, your CAS from a licensed student sponsor, academic transcripts, IELTS UKVI or an accepted alternative, financial evidence held for 28 days, and a tuberculosis test certificate from an IOM-approved clinic in Dhaka. Some science and engineering postgraduates also need an ATAS certificate. SA Associates prepares the full file.
What IELTS score do I need for UK?
Two things apply. For the visa, degree-level study requires CEFR B2 in all four skills. For admission, universities usually ask for IELTS UKVI 6.0–6.5 overall, rising to 7.0+ for law, medicine and the most competitive courses. If you fall just short, a pre-sessional English course at the same university is often the cleanest fix.
How much money do I need to show for a UK student visa?
First-year tuition, less any deposit already paid, plus living costs for up to nine months. The monthly living-cost threshold is set higher for London than for the rest of the UK and is revised periodically, so confirm the current figure on GOV.UK before you apply. The strict part is the 28-day rule: the full amount must sit in the account for 28 consecutive days, and the closing balance must be dated within 31 days of your application.
Which intake is best for Bangladeshi students applying to UK?
The September (Autumn) intake is the main one by a wide margin — almost every course runs, and scholarship rounds are open. Start about 12 months ahead. January is a genuine second option with a decent course list. May is limited to a handful of programmes at a small number of universities.
Can my spouse come with me to UK?
Only for certain courses. Since January 2024, dependants may accompany PhD and other research postgraduate students, and government-sponsored students. If you are doing a taught master's or an undergraduate degree, you cannot bring a spouse or children. Where dependants are allowed, they do get full, unrestricted work rights — which is better than the US F-2 position.
How long does the UK student visa take from Bangladesh?
Standard processing runs around three weeks from your biometrics appointment, and a priority service is available at extra cost. The queue lengthens sharply in July and August, so aim to apply as soon as your CAS is issued rather than close to your course start date.
What is the Graduate Route and how long does it last?
The Graduate Route lets you stay and work in the UK after your degree with no job offer and no employer sponsorship. It currently runs two years for bachelor's and master's graduates and three for PhDs. From 1 January 2027 the bachelor's and master's allowance drops to 18 months, so if you are finishing in 2026 it is worth applying before 31 December to keep the full two years. You must apply from inside the UK, and it cannot be extended.
Can I settle permanently in UK after studying?
It is possible, and unlike the US there is no lottery involved. The usual path is Graduate Route, then a Skilled Worker visa with a licensed employer, then Indefinite Leave to Remain after a qualifying period. What it does require is an employer willing to sponsor you and a role that meets the salary threshold, so your field and your job search matter a great deal.
Are there scholarships available for Bangladeshi students in UK?
Yes. Chevening is the flagship UK government award for master's study and is open to Bangladeshi applicants, alongside Commonwealth Scholarships. Almost every university also runs its own international merit awards, typically a partial tuition discount of 10–50%. UK funding tends to be less generous than US assistantships, so we plan around a realistic contribution rather than full coverage.
Should I choose UK over Canada or USA?
The UK's strongest card is speed: a one-year master's means one year of fees and living costs instead of two, and you start working sooner. It also has the shortest, simplest visa process of the three. Against that, the Graduate Route is shorter than Canada's post-study permit and is shortening further in 2027, and settlement depends on finding a sponsoring employer. Choose the UK for speed and reputation, Canada for a clearer residency path, the USA for research funding. We will give you an honest comparison for your profile.
