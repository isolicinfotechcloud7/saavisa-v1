Why Australia

Why Bangladeshi Students Choose Australia

Eight reasons Australia offers the strongest combination
of ranking, work rights and migration pathway.

Nine in the Global Top 100

Melbourne at #19 and UNSW at #20 both outrank Yale and Princeton, and nine Australian universities sit inside the QS global top 100.

Up to 4 Years of Work

The Temporary Graduate visa lets eligible graduates stay and work for two to four years depending on qualification level and where you studied.

Clear Migration Path

A points-based skilled migration system with state and regional nomination streams — no employer lottery, and post-study work experience counts.

Work 48 Hours a Fortnight

Work rights are among the most generous anywhere — 48 hours a fortnight in term time and unlimited hours during official course breaks.

Bangladeshi Community

Well-established Bangladeshi communities in Sydney and Melbourne, with active student associations and cultural organisations on most campuses.

Regional Study Bonus

Studying outside Sydney, Melbourne and Brisbane can earn an extra year of post-study work and additional points towards skilled migration.

Strong Graduate Salaries

Australia has one of the highest minimum wages in the world, so part-time work covers more of your living costs than it would elsewhere.

Safe, Liveable Cities

Melbourne, Sydney, Adelaide and Perth all rank among the world’s most liveable cities, with strong student protections written into law.

What You Need

Admission Requirements

Complete checklist for Bangladeshi students
applying to Australian universities.

Academic Requirements

  • HSC with strong grades for direct undergraduate entry; a foundation or diploma pathway is common after HSC
  • Bachelor's with roughly CGPA 2.8–3.0+ for most coursework master's programmes
  • Completed SSC, HSC and Bachelor's transcripts (translated & attested)
  • GRE/GMAT rarely required — most Australian master's programmes do not ask for them
  • Work experience letters, valued highly for MBA and professional master's entry
  • Statement of Purpose, which doubles as the basis of your Genuine Student statement

English Proficiency

  • IELTS Academic: 6.0 overall with no band below 5.5 is the visa minimum for a Subclass 500
  • Universities usually ask for 6.5 overall, rising to 7.0+ for nursing, teaching and law
  • The Temporary Graduate visa later requires 6.5 overall — plan for that score now
  • PTE Academic and TOEFL iBT widely accepted; Duolingo accepted by some providers
  • ELICOS English packaging available if you fall short of the entry score

Financial Requirements

  • Living costs of AUD 29,710 for 12 months, plus your full first-year tuition and return airfare
  • Funds must be genuine, legally sourced and traceable — sudden large deposits are a common refusal trigger
  • Held for at least three months in most cases; an education loan sanction letter is strong evidence
  • Sponsor income proof, tax returns and relationship documents where a parent or relative funds you
  • Scholarship or university award letters, where applicable
  • Bangladesh sits in a higher-scrutiny evidence category, so financial documentation is examined closely

Other Documents

  • Valid passport with at least six months validity
  • CoE (Confirmation of Enrolment) from a CRICOS-registered provider
  • Genuine Student (GS) statement — the single most important document in the application
  • OSHC policy covering the full duration of your visa
  • Health examination at a panel clinic in Dhaka, and police clearance where requested
  • Biometrics at the Australian Visa Application Centre in Dhaka
  • CV and work experience letters for postgraduate applications

What You Can Do During & After Studies

What Subclass 500 holders are permitted to do
during their studies and after graduating.

During Studies

48 hrs/fortnight

48 hours a fortnight while your course is in session and unlimited hours during official course breaks. Masters by Research and PhD students have no cap at any time.

After Graduation

2 to 4 years

The Temporary Graduate visa (subclass 485) gives full, unrestricted work rights for two to four years depending on your qualification. Studying in a regional area can add further time.

Longer-Term Route

Points-Tested PR

Australia’s skilled migration system is points-tested rather than employer-controlled: a skills assessment, an expression of interest through SkillSelect, then a 189, 190 or 491 invitation. Australian study, regional study and post-study work experience all add points.

FAQ

Common Questions About Studying in Australia

Quick answers to common questions
from Bangladeshi students.

What documents are required for an Australian student visa from Bangladesh?
A valid passport, your Confirmation of Enrolment from a CRICOS-registered provider, academic transcripts, IELTS or an accepted alternative, OSHC cover, financial evidence, a health examination, and your Genuine Student statement. The GS statement is the document most applications are refused on, and it is where we spend the most time.
What IELTS score do I need for Australia?
The visa minimum for a Subclass 500 is IELTS 6.0 overall with no band below 5.5. Universities usually ask for 6.5 overall, and nursing, teaching and law commonly require 7.0. Plan for 6.5 from the start, because the Temporary Graduate visa later requires 6.5 too — students who scrape in at 6.0 often find themselves retaking the test three years later.
How much money do I need to show for an Australian student visa?
AUD 29,710 for twelve months of living costs, plus your full first-year tuition and return airfare. For a student with AUD 40,000 tuition that is roughly AUD 72,000 in total. What matters as much as the amount is the source: funds must be genuine, legally earned and traceable. A balance that appears suddenly and disappears after the grant is the single most common reason Bangladeshi applications are refused.
Which intake is best for Bangladeshi students applying to Australia?
February (Semester 1) is the main intake — nearly every course runs and scholarship rounds are open. Start about 12 months ahead, which means beginning in the previous February or March. July (Semester 2) is a solid second option at most universities. The November summer term is limited to a small number of courses.
Can my spouse come with me to Australia?
Yes. Unlike the UK, Australia allows dependants on the Subclass 500 for coursework students as well as research students. Your spouse can work 48 hours a fortnight, and the spouse of a Masters by Research or PhD student has unlimited work rights. You must show additional funds for each dependant and buy OSHC for them, and school-age children may attract tuition fees.
How long does the Australian student visa take from Bangladesh?
Processing commonly runs from four weeks to several months, and Bangladesh sits in a higher-scrutiny evidence category, so files are examined more closely than for many other countries. Lodge as soon as your CoE is issued — ideally three to four months before your course starts — and expect the Department to request further documents.
What is the 485 visa and how long does it last?
The Temporary Graduate visa (subclass 485) gives eligible graduates full work rights for two to four years, depending on your qualification level. Studying in a regional area can extend it further. You need IELTS 6.5, you must be under the age limit, and you must apply from inside Australia. The application charge is substantial and non-refundable, so budget for it from the start rather than treating it as an afterthought.
Can I settle permanently in Australia after studying?
Australia has the clearest pathway of the major destinations, because it is points-tested rather than employer-controlled. The usual route is Subclass 500, then the 485 graduate visa, then a skills assessment and an expression of interest through SkillSelect, leading to a 189, 190 or 491 invitation. Your occupation, age, English score and whether you studied regionally all affect your points. It is competitive, not automatic — but you are not dependent on one employer saying yes.
Are there scholarships available for Bangladeshi students in Australia?
Yes. Australia Awards is the flagship government scholarship and is open to Bangladeshi applicants, covering full tuition and living costs for selected candidates. Beyond that, almost every university runs international merit scholarships worth roughly 10–50% of tuition, and Destination Australia supports study in regional areas. We plan around a realistic partial award rather than assuming full coverage.
Should I choose Australia over Canada or UK?
Australia's strongest cards are work rights and the migration pathway: 48 hours a fortnight while studying, up to four years afterwards, and a points system that does not depend on an employer sponsoring you. Wages are high enough that part-time work genuinely offsets living costs. Against that, tuition and living costs are among the higher-cost options across the five destinations, degrees run two years rather than the UK's one, and the visa is scrutinised heavily for Bangladeshi applicants. Choose Australia for the residency pathway, the UK for speed, Canada for lower cost. We will give you an honest comparison for your profile.
