What You Need

Admission Requirements

Complete checklist for Bangladeshi students
applying to Canadian universities.

Academic Requirements

  • Minimum GPA 3.0/4.0 for Bachelor's; 3.3+ preferred for Master's
  • Completed SSC, HSC, and Bachelor's transcripts (translated & attested)
  • Equivalence certificate from your education board
  • Some programs require GRE/GMAT (engineering, MBA)
  • 2-3 Letters of Recommendation (LOR)
  • Strong Statement of Purpose (SOP)

English Proficiency

  • IELTS Academic: Minimum 6.0 overall, 6.5+ preferred (no band < 5.5)
  • TOEFL iBT: 80+ overall, 90+ for top universities
  • PTE Academic: 58+ overall, 65+ preferred
  • Duolingo English Test: 110+ accepted at many universities
  • MOI Certificate accepted at select universities

Financial Requirements

  • Tuition coverage: CAD 20,000-35,000 (program-dependent)
  • Living expenses proof: CAD 23,448 minimum (updated for applications on/after Sept 1, 2026)
  • Recent bank statements / financial documents showing stable and sufficient funds
  • Guaranteed Investment Certificate (GIC) or other acceptable proof of funds: CAD 23,448 living-cost proof + tuition and travel
  • Sponsor letter (if funded by parent/relative)
  • Tax returns of sponsor (last 2 years)

Other Documents

  • Valid passport (min 6 months validity beyond stay)
  • Passport-sized photographs (white background)
  • Police clearance certificate
  • Medical examination report (from IRCC-approved panel)
  • Letter of Acceptance from a Designated Learning Institution (DLI)
  • Provincial/Territorial Attestation Letter (PAL/TAL), unless exempt
  • Resume/CV (for graduate applications)

FAQ

Common Questions About Studying in Canada

Quick answers to common questions
from Bangladeshi students.

What documents are required for a Canada student visa from Bangladesh?
You need a valid passport, Letter of Acceptance (LOA) from a Designated Learning Institution, proof of funds, academic transcripts, IELTS/TOEFL score, biometrics, medical exam, and a Statement of Purpose. SA Associates handles the complete documentation.
What is the minimum IELTS score required for a Canada student visa?
There is no active SDS pathway now. English requirements mainly depend on the institution and program. Many universities commonly ask for IELTS Academic around 6.0–6.5 overall, but top programs may require higher. TOEFL, PTE, and Duolingo may also be accepted depending on the university.
How much bank balance is required for a Canada student visa from Bangladesh?

For applications submitted on or after 1 September 2026, a single student studying in Canada outside Quebec must show at least CAD 23,448 for first-year living expenses, plus first-year tuition fees and transportation costs.

For example, if your first-year tuition is CAD 20,000, you may need to demonstrate around CAD 43,448 plus transportation costs. Tuition already paid can be supported with payment receipts.

IRCC also considers the source and availability of your funds, so bank statements and supporting financial documents should clearly show that the required money is genuinely available.

The required amount is higher if family members accompany you, while Quebec follows separate financial requirements.

SA Associates can help you calculate the exact amount required and prepare the right financial documents for your Canada study permit application.

Which intake is best for Bangladeshi students applying to Canada?
The Fall (September) intake is best — widest program selection, more scholarships, and standard visa processing time. Start your application 9-12 months ahead. Winter (January) is the second-best option. Summer (May) has limited programs.
Can my spouse work in Canada while I study?
Yes — if you are enrolled in a graduate program (Master's or PhD) at a Canadian DLI, your spouse can apply for an Open Work Permit (OWP) and work full-time for any employer in Canada. Recent IRCC updates have tightened eligibility for undergraduate spouses.
How long does Canada student visa processing take from Bangladesh?
Study permit processing times change regularly and depend on application volume, completeness, biometrics, medicals, and IRCC review. SA Associates helps prepare a complete, decision-ready file to reduce avoidable delays.
What is the Post-Graduation Work Permit (PGWP) and who is eligible?
PGWP may allow graduates of eligible Canadian DLIs to work in Canada for up to 3 years after completing an eligible program. To qualify, students must meet IRCC rules, apply within 180 days of receiving final marks, and satisfy language and field-of-study requirements where applicable.
Can I apply for Canadian PR after my studies?
Yes! After graduating and gaining 1 year of skilled Canadian work experience (via PGWP), you can apply through Express Entry's Canadian Experience Class (CEC) or various Provincial Nominee Programs (PNP). Many of our students secure PR within 3-4 years of arriving in Canada.
Are there scholarships available for Bangladeshi students in Canada?
Yes — most Canadian universities offer entrance scholarships ranging from CAD 1,000 to CAD 30,000 based on academic merit. Top scholarships include Vanier CGS (PhD), Lester B. Pearson (UofT), and Schulich Leader Scholarships (engineering/science). SA Associates helps identify and apply for these.
Is the Student Direct Stream (SDS) still available?
No. The Student Direct Stream ended on November 8, 2024. New study permit applications are now processed through the regular study permit application process, with complete documents, proof of funds, biometrics, and PAL/TAL where required.
