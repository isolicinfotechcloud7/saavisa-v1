What You Need

Admission Requirements

Complete checklist for Bangladeshi students
applying to US universities.

Academic Requirements

  • Minimum GPA 3.0/4.0 for Bachelor's; 3.0–3.5+ for competitive Master's programs
  • Completed SSC, HSC, and Bachelor's transcripts (translated & attested)
  • SAT or ACT for some undergraduate programs — many are now test-optional
  • GRE for most graduate programs; GMAT for MBA (waivers increasingly common)
  • 2–3 Letters of Recommendation (LOR)
  • Statement of Purpose and, for undergraduates, personal essays

English Proficiency

  • TOEFL iBT: 79–80+ typical, 100+ for the most competitive universities
  • IELTS Academic: 6.5 overall typical, 7.0+ for top programs
  • Duolingo English Test: 110+ accepted at a growing number of universities
  • PTE Academic: 58+ overall, 65+ preferred
  • Requirements vary widely by university — always confirm the specific program

Financial Requirements

  • Proof of funds covering the full first year shown on your I-20 (tuition + living costs)
  • Bank statements showing stable, seasoned funds — sudden large deposits raise questions
  • Affidavit of Support and sponsor bank documents (if funded by parent or relative)
  • Sponsor income proof and tax returns (last 2–3 years)
  • Scholarship or assistantship award letters, where applicable
  • Education loan sanction letter, if part of your funding

Other Documents

  • Valid passport (min 6 months validity beyond your intended stay)
  • Form I-20 issued by an SEVP-certified university
  • I-901 SEVIS fee receipt and DS-160 confirmation page
  • Visa interview appointment confirmation (US Embassy Dhaka)
  • Digital photograph meeting US visa specifications
  • Resume/CV and research or work experience documents (graduate applications)
  • Evidence of ties to Bangladesh and intent to return after study

Work Rights

What You Can Do During & After Studies

What F-1 students are permitted to do
during their studies and after graduating.

During Studies

20 hrs/week

On-campus work during term time, and full-time during official breaks. CPT allows off-campus work tied to your course, usually after the first academic year.

After Graduation

OPT up to 3 years

Optional Practical Training gives 12 months of work authorisation in your field. Graduates of designated STEM programs can apply for a further 24 months, making 36 in total.

Longer-Term Route

H-1B Sponsorship

Many graduates move from OPT to an employer-sponsored H-1B work visa, which can later support a green card application. Unlike Canada, the US has no direct study-to-residency route — sponsorship and the annual H-1B lottery both apply.

FAQ

Common Questions About Studying in USA

Quick answers to common questions
from Bangladeshi students.

What documents are required for a US student visa from Bangladesh?
A valid passport, your Form I-20 from an SEVP-certified university, the I-901 SEVIS fee receipt, DS-160 confirmation, interview appointment letter, academic transcripts, TOEFL or IELTS scores, and financial documents proving you can cover the first year shown on the I-20. SA Associates prepares the full file.
What English test score do US universities require?
There is no visa-level English requirement — the score is set by each university. TOEFL iBT around 79–80 or IELTS 6.5 is typical, while the most competitive programs ask for TOEFL 100+ or IELTS 7.0+. Duolingo and PTE are accepted at a growing number of institutions. Always check the specific program page.
How much money do I need to show for a US student visa?
There is no single fixed figure. You must show funds covering the full first-year cost printed on your I-20 — tuition plus living expenses — which commonly falls between USD 45,000 and 82,000 depending on the university. Consular officers look for stable, well-documented funds rather than a large deposit made shortly before the interview.
Which intake is best for Bangladeshi students applying to USA?
Fall (August) is the main intake — the widest program choice, the most funding, and the largest assistantship pool. Start 12 months ahead. Spring (January) is a solid second option with fewer scholarships. Summer (May) is limited to a small number of programs.
Can my spouse work in USA while I study?
No. Your spouse can join you on an F-2 dependent visa, but F-2 holders are not permitted to work in the United States and may only study part-time. This is a significant difference from Canada, where the spouses of graduate students can often obtain an open work permit.
How long does the US student visa process take from Bangladesh?
The interview decision is usually given on the day, but the wait for an appointment slot at the US Embassy in Dhaka is the real variable and can stretch to several months in peak season. Book your slot as early as your I-20 allows. Administrative processing after the interview can add further weeks in some cases.
What is OPT and how long can I work after graduating?
Optional Practical Training gives eligible F-1 graduates 12 months of work authorisation in their field of study. Graduates of designated STEM programs may apply for a further 24-month extension, bringing the total to 36 months. You apply through your university's international office, normally before you graduate.
Can I get permanent residency in USA after studying?
It is possible but not automatic, and it takes longer than in Canada. The common route is OPT, then an employer-sponsored H-1B work visa, then an employer-sponsored green card. The H-1B is subject to an annual lottery, so outcomes depend on your employer, your field and timing. We are candid with students about this from the start.
Are there scholarships available for Bangladeshi students in USA?
Yes, and US funding is often more substantial than elsewhere. Undergraduates can receive merit scholarships and need-based aid; graduate students frequently secure teaching or research assistantships that cover tuition and pay a stipend. Fulbright is also open to Bangladeshi applicants. We help identify realistic targets and build the application.
Is studying in USA worth it compared to Canada?
It depends on your goal. The USA leads on university ranking, research funding and graduate assistantships, and starting salaries are typically higher. Canada is cheaper and offers a clearer route to permanent residency. If your priority is academic reputation and earnings, the USA is strong; if it is settling permanently, Canada is more predictable. We will give you an honest comparison for your profile.
