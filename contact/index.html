Before You Reach Out

Common questions from prospective students — answered briefly here.

Is the consultation really free?

Yes, 100%. Our first counseling session is completely free with no obligation to sign up for any service.

Do I need an appointment?

Yes — we recommend booking ahead via WhatsApp so we can assign the right counselor and dedicate proper time to your case.

What documents should I bring?

Just your academic transcripts, passport (if available), and English test score. We'll guide you on the rest.

How fast do you respond?

WhatsApp messages within 10 minutes during office hours. We'll connect you with the right specialist quickly.
